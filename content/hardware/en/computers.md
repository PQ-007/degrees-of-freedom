---
title: "Microcontrollers and computers"
summary: "The robot's brains: from a chip that runs one program to computers that run Linux."
---

Start from what the robot has to think about:

- **Learning, or a simple robot with a few sensors and motors?** An 8-bit board.
- **Several motors to keep in time, many sensors, buses?** A 32-bit microcontroller.
- **Driven from a phone or a laptop?** A wireless microcontroller.
- **A drone?** A flight controller, which comes with its motion sensors.
- **Cameras, maps, ROS?** A single-board computer.
- **Neural networks on camera images, on the robot itself?** An AI computer.

Many robots use two: a computer that thinks, and a [[~microcontroller|microcontroller]] beside the motors that keeps them in time.
