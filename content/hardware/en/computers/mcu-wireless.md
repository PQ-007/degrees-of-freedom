- **Good for:** robots driven from a phone or a laptop, and sending sensor data over the air.
- **Not for:** jobs that need a full computer.
- **Typical:** Wi-Fi and Bluetooth on the same chip as the processor.
- **Topic:** [[C12]]

Putting the radio on the same chip as the processor saves a separate module and its wiring. The robot can serve a web page, take commands from a phone, or stream its sensor readings to a laptop.

<aside class="examples" aria-label="Common models">

**Common models** · checked 14 November 2026

- **ESP32:** one or two Xtensa LX6 cores at up to 240 MHz, 520 KB of SRAM, Wi-Fi 802.11 b/g/n, and Bluetooth v4.2 with Bluetooth LE.[@esp32]

</aside>
