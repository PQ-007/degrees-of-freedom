- **Good for:** recognising objects, following lines and markers, seeing colour.
- **Not for:** distance from a single image, or darkness and glare.
- **Typical:** millions of pixels, tens of frames a second.
- **Topic:** [[C10]]

A camera measures the light falling on millions of pixels at once. That holds more about the world than any other sensor, but turning it into "a cup, 40 cm to the left" is the hard part: that is [[P5]] and [[P6]].
