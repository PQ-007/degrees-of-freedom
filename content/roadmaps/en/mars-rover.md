## How to use this roadmap {#how}

Work down the path in order: each topic builds on the ones above it. **Must** topics are the core. **Helpful** ones deepen it, and can wait if you are keen to start building. Tick a topic when you have learned it; your ticks are kept in this browser, and the topic pages show them too.

The folded **Later** topics are for when your rover works and you want it to map the ground and plan its own routes across it.

## Then build it {#build}

The Builds section will include a small rocker-bogie rover: six gearmotors, a microcontroller, a battery and a camera, enough to try most of the must topics for real. The teardown of a planetary rover shows what a full-size one is made of.
